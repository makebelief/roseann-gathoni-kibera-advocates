FOUNDER & PRINCIPAL

Roseann Gathoni

Roseann Gathoni is the founder and principal of Roseann Gathoni Kibera Advocates. She brings over a decade of experience within Kenya’s legal ecosystem, including more than 11 years at Kimani Kaimwa & Co. Advocates, where she served as a Senior Associate and Legal Strategist.

Her work spans corporate structures, investor readiness and legal frameworks that help growing companies manage risk and make better decisions.

Throughout her career, she has worked on investor-ready systems for more than 100 founders across East Africa, as well as legal-technology frameworks and risk audit tools.

She established the firm to help organisations strengthen their underlying legal and institutional systems as they scale.
